short: Birth plans, provider matching & weekly pregnancy guidance
full: True Joy Birthing helps you plan, prepare, and navigate your pregnancy and birth with the people you trust.

BUILD YOUR BIRTH PLAN
Answer guided questions and create a shareable birth plan your care team can actually use. Export a clean PDF for your provider file.

FIND AND INVITE PROVIDERS
Search doulas and midwives, share your birth plan, and message directly in the app. Providers can accept or decline your invite from chat.

STAY ON TRACK
Weekly tips and affirmations matched to your week of pregnancy, a personal timeline, and simple check-ins to reflect on how you're feeling.

FOR YOUR CARE TEAM
Doulas, midwives, and lactation professionals get their own workspace: clients, appointments, contracts, invoices, notes, and clinical forms.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
Your birth plan is shared only with the providers you choose. Delete your account at any time from settings.

PRO SUBSCRIPTION (FOR PROVIDERS)
The core app is free for mothers. True Joy Pro — $29.99/month or $274.99/year — gives doulas, midwives, and lactation professionals client management and history, digital contracts with e-signatures, professional invoicing, detailed client notes and visit summaries, and marketplace profile visibility. Subscriptions renew automatically; cancel anytime in Google Play subscriptions.

Weekly Tip
Week 28
With the third trimester beginning and baby's lungs and brain rapidly maturing, make it a habit to notice your baby's movement pattern daily and learn what 'normal for your baby' feels like, because by 28 weeks more than 90% of babies could survive with advanced care if born early, and changes in movement are one of the first signs midwives and doctors want you to call about—not something to ignore.

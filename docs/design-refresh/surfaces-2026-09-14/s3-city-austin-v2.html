Where You'll Give Birth in Austin

Hospitals & birth centers, honestly

The real logistics from someone who's walked these halls with laboring moms — entrances, parking, NICU levels, and how doulas are treated.
St. David's South Austin
901 W Ben White Blvd · NICU III · Medicaid ✓
One of the busiest L&D units in the city. Doulas welcome as part of your support team — bring your birth plan; the conversation goes easier with preferences written down. Tours run twice monthly; ask about the natural birth-friendly rooms on the north wing.
Seton Medical Center Austin
1201 W 38th St · NICU III · Medicaid ✓
Central Austin near the UT campus, part of the Ascension/Seton system with Dell Children's right next door. A doula who knows your hospital's rhythm makes a real difference in labor — and Seton's lactation team is on staff seven days a week.
Medicaid & Insurance in Texas

Cost help, without the runaround

Texas Medicaid covers doula services as of 2024 — prenatal, labor, and postpartum support. Contact your Medicaid managed care plan to find an enrolled doula near you. Private plans vary; HSA/FSA funds can often help cover out-of-pocket costs, and several Austin practices offer sliding-scale rates.

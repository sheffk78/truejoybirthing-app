Medicaid & Insurance in Texas

Cost help, without the runaround

Texas Medicaid covers doula services as of 2024 — prenatal, labor, and postpartum support. Contact your Medicaid managed care plan to find an enrolled doula near you. Private plans vary; HSA/FSA funds can often help cover out-of-pocket costs.

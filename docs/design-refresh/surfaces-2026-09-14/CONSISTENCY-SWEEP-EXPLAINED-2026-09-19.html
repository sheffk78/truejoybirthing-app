3.Nine icons existed in the app but were missing from the design binder

The app's icon set (left artwork) was missing nine glyphs from the design documentation copy — every icon the app actually draws, including these, is now recorded. The app itself never showed a wrong icon; this was bookkeeping so future screens (and other designers/agents) pull from the same complete set.
The nine: History (archive shelf) · Add (plus) · Share (arrow out of tray) · Settings (gear) · Timeline (arch) · Notifications (bell) · Contract (document with seal) · Invoice (document with lines) · Invoice Paid (check circle). Drawn at 24×24, 1.7 stroke — same hand-drawn thin-line style as the rest of the set.
Also cleaned: two small code-hygiene items that kept the build green — a dead import removed, and a style property that used a web-only spelling corrected to the app's valid spelling (translate → translateY, used for a half-pixel optical alignment on My Team's status chips).
Source of truth: TIcon.tsx (what the app renders) ↔ icons.mjs (design binder). Now 31/31, verified programmatically.

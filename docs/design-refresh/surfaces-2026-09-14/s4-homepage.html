Homepage — what this surface proves
  • Scale-up test: the app language carries the brand's face — serif display headline, illustration hero, unified palette (production type system confirmed: Cormorant Garamond + Source Sans 3).
  • The motif becomes the story: the 4-stage growth strip (sprout → leafing → budding → blossom, weeks mapped) turns Jeff's growth ruling into a brand narrative section — vegetation only.
  • Feature cards with motif icons: hand-drawn sprig/lavender stem icons instead of stock lucide glyphs.
  • Real assets: real logo, real product booklet mockup, approved week-28 illustration. No AI-generated people.
